Thank you!

Thank you for visiting FNAF Fan! This website was created as a celebration of Five Nights at Freddy’s and the incredible community that has grown around it over the years. Whether you’ve been here since the doors of Freddy Fazbear’s Pizza first opened or you’ve only recently discovered the series, I hope this site gives you a fun place to explore the timelines, characters, fangames, resources, and everything else this franchise has to offer. FNAF Fan is a fan-made project built with appreciation for the games, their creators, and all the fans who continue to keep the mystery alive.

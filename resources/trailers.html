Five Nights at Freddy's

The game that started it all. Five Nights at Freddy's puts you in the role of a night security guard at Freddy Fazbear's Pizza, where surviving until 6 AM means keeping a close eye on the restaurant's animatronic performers.

Five Nights at Freddy's 2

A new and improved Freddy Fazbear's Pizza brings a larger cast of animatronics and a very different kind of night shift. With no doors to protect you, survival depends on quick reactions, cameras, and a familiar Freddy mask.

Five Nights at Freddy's 3

Thirty years after Freddy Fazbear's Pizza closed its doors, the legend returns as a horror attraction. Among old relics and faulty equipment, one very real animatronic still remains.

Five Nights at Freddy's 4

The nightmare comes home. Trapped in a dark bedroom, you must listen carefully and defend yourself against terrifying versions of familiar animatronics until morning.

Five Nights at Freddy's World

A colorful RPG adventure where characters from across the FNAF series join forces in a strange and unpredictable world. Behind its cheerful appearance, however, things are not quite as simple as they seem.

Five Nights at Freddy's Sister Location

Welcome to Circus Baby's Entertainment and Rental, where advanced animatronics await beneath the surface. As a late-night technician, your tasks quickly become far more dangerous than routine maintenance.

Five Nights at Freddy's Pizzeria Simulator

Build and manage your own Freddy Fazbear's Pizza by day, then survive what lurks inside after closing time. What begins as a simple business simulator soon reveals a much darker purpose.

Five Nights at Freddy's Ultimate Custom Night

The ultimate FNAF challenge puts dozens of animatronics under your control. Customize your opponents, adjust their difficulty, and survive a night built entirely around chaos.

Five Nights at Freddy's Help Wanted

Fazbear Entertainment returns with a collection of virtual reality experiences inspired by its questionable past. Repair animatronics, survive classic nights, and discover what may be hiding inside the game itself.

Five Nights at Freddy's AR: Special Delivery

Fazbear Entertainment brings its animatronics directly to your home through its new delivery service. Unfortunately, malfunctioning characters turn the experience into a fight for survival in the real world.

Five Nights at Freddy's Security Breach

Trapped overnight inside Freddy Fazbear's Mega Pizzaplex, Gregory must survive until morning with the unexpected help of Glamrock Freddy. Explore the massive complex while avoiding its relentless animatronic inhabitants.

Five Nights at Freddy's Security Breach: Ruin

Return to the abandoned and shattered Mega Pizzaplex as Cassie, searching for her friend Gregory beneath the ruins. With the V.A.N.N.I. mask revealing a hidden digital world, the Pizzaplex holds new threats and deeper secrets.

Five Nights at Freddy's Help Wanted 2

Return to Fazbear Entertainment as its newest employee and prove you have what it takes to handle the job. A new collection of minigames puts your skills to the test across familiar locations, attractions, and animatronics.

Five Nights at Freddy's The Secret of The Mimic

Enter the abandoned workshop of Murray's Costume Manor and uncover the secrets left behind by its mysterious inventor. Deep inside waits the Mimic, a prototype capable of becoming almost anything.
